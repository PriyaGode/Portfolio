~ About Me i am working on so and so ~

As the daughter of an industrial designer, I naturally developed a deep appreciation for design and technology early on. From a young age, I found myself exploring the inner workings of websites and digital designs, which led me to freelance as a web designer and developer while still in school. This passion grew steadily, and several years ago, I discovered Webflow. Instantly captivated by its capabilities, I dedicated myself fully to mastering it. Over the past 10+ years, I’ve worked full-time as an independent Webflow developer, collaborating with clients I once only dreamed of. Honesty and transparency are at the core of my work ethic, driving me to create remarkable solutions alongside teams that share the same commitment.

With a solid foundation in full-stack development, cloud technologies, and Salesforce CRM, I am proficient in Java, JavaScript, Angular, and ReactJS, complemented by hands-on experience with Spring Boot, Hibernate, and AWS. I have a proven record of designing and delivering scalable, secure applications, optimizing workflows, and implementing improvements that enhance system performance. My Master’s degree in Computer Science from Oklahoma City University has further strengthened my technical expertise.

I am dedicated to continuous learning and professional growth, always aiming to deliver high-quality software that aligns with client goals. Whether developing cloud-native applications or crafting engaging user experiences, I am passionate about innovation, excellence, and collaborating with teams that value transparency and shared success.
